**Task 2: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer retention and churn patterns using the Telco Customer Churn dataset.

The main objective is to understand why customers leave a subscription-based service, identify customer segments with higher churn rates, analyze customer retention, and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer loyalty.

The analysis is developed using Microsoft Power BI to transform customer data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention rates.
- Identify customer segments with high churn.
- Understand the impact of contract types on customer retention.
- Analyze customer tenure and lifetime patterns.
- Identify important factors influencing customer churn.
- Provide practical recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Telco Customer Churn Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn

The dataset contains customer information, subscription details, services, payment methods, monthly charges, total charges, tenure, and churn status.

## 📈 Key Business Metrics

The project focuses on the following metrics:

**Churn Rate**

Percentage of customers who discontinued the service.

**Retention Rate**

Percentage of customers who continue using the service.

**Customer Tenure**

Length of time a customer has remained subscribed.

**Customer Lifetime Value**

An estimate of the revenue associated with a customer over their relationship with the business.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Customer Onboarding:** Introduce onboarding support and engagement programs for new customers.

2. **Encourage Long-Term Contracts:** Offer incentives to customers who move from monthly subscriptions to annual plans.

3. **Improve Technical Support:** Strengthen customer support services to improve satisfaction.

4. **Promote Online Security:** Provide attractive security service packages.

5. **Monitor High-Risk Customers:** Identify customer segments with higher churn and introduce targeted retention campaigns.

6. **Improve Customer Engagement:** Use personalized offers and loyalty programs to encourage long-term subscriptions.
